# Intent Statement

## Problem Statement

- Teams running AI-DLC on Kiro have no way to see how many Kiro credits each AI-DLC stage uses. [desc] [Q3]
- The intent is a Kiro extension skill that counts the credits used by each AI-DLC stage. [desc]

## Target Customer

- The users are the team members who run AI-DLC on Kiro; the requester is one user among several. [Q5]
- A team lead or budget owner decides what is built. [Q3]
- The pain is wanting to know what AI-DLC runs cost in Kiro credits. [Q1]

## Success Metrics

- The skill shows per-stage credits live or at each stage gate while a run is going. [Q2]

## Initiative Trigger

- Credit costs became a concern for the team. [Q3]

## Initial Scope Signal

- Workflow-selected scope: `poc` (workflow-selected). [scope]
- User-confirmed product boundary: nothing is ruled out of the first version yet. [Q4]
